REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open js/data.js.
2. Replace SITE.brand with your real agency / brand name.
3. Set SITE.whatsappNumber to your WhatsApp Business number in international digits-only format.
   Bangladesh example format: 8801XXXXXXXXX (replace X with your actual number; do not include +, spaces or dashes).
4. Add your public contact number to SITE.hotline if you want it shown.
5. Add your business email to SITE.email.
6. Review every university programme and fee range in js/data.js against current official university information. The current fee ranges are still sample estimates and must not be advertised as verified quotations.
7. Confirm the exact services you provide, service charges (if any), partner status and current visa guidance before publishing those claims.

HOW THE WHATSAPP FORM WORKS
- The student completes the enquiry form.
- The website opens WhatsApp with a prepared message containing the student's answers.
- The student reviews and presses Send in WhatsApp.
- This static website does not silently store submissions or send messages in the background. If you later need a lead dashboard, CRM, Google Sheets integration or analytics, connect a secure backend/service.

RUN LOCALLY
- Extract the ZIP.
- Open index.html in a modern browser.
- For deployment, upload the whole folder structure (index.html, css/, js/, images/) to your hosting provider.
